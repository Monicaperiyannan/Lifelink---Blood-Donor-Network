Overview

Welcome to LifeLink — Blood Donor Network. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

LifeLink is a final-year academic project built to connect blood donors with patients in need across India. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

⚠️ Academic Project Notice: LifeLink is built as a Final Year Project and is NOT intended for commercial use. All data submitted is used solely for demonstration purposes within the scope of this educational project.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Data Type What We Collect Required?
Account Info Username, email, password (hashed) Yes
Personal Info Full name, phone number, city Yes
Health Info Blood group, age, weight, last donation date Yes (for donors)
Location Info City and state Yes
Email Address For account identification Optional
Usage Data Pages visited, search queries Automatic

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Create and manage your LifeLink donor or user account
  • Enable blood donor search and location-based matching
  • Process emergency blood requests
  • Check and display donation eligibility
  • Send important notifications about blood requests (if applicable)
  • Generate anonymous analytics for blood bank statistics
  • Improve platform features and user experience
  • Ensure platform security and prevent misuse
We do NOT: Sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with third parties for commercial purposes, or use it beyond the scope of this project.

Information Sharing

LifeLink does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with external parties. The following visibility rules apply:

Information Visible To Hidden From
Name, Blood Group, City Logged-in users Public guests
Phone Number Logged-in users (search results) Public, non-users
Email Address Admin only Other users
Password Never stored in plain text Everyone (hashed)
Emergency Requests Admin, Request owner Other users

Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

Password Hashing

All passwords are hashed using Werkzeug's secure generate_password_hash — never stored as plain text.

Session Management

Flask server-side sessions protect authenticated routes from unauthorized access.

Role-Based Access

Separate user and admin roles with decorator-based access control on every route.

SQLite Database

Data is stored locally in a secure SQLite database with parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection.

Your Rights

As a LifeLink user, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: View your profile and donor registration details anytime.
  • Right to Update: Edit your profile information and donor details.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting support@lifelink.health.
  • Right to Withdraw: Mark yourself as unavailable to stop appearing in donor searches.
  • Right to Transparency: This privacy policy explains exactly how your data is used.

Terms & Conditions

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using LifeLink, you agree to these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree, please do not use this platform.

2. User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and truthful information during registration.
  • Keep your login credentials secure and confidential.
  • Do not submit false emergency blood requests.
  • Do not attempt to access admin or other users' data.
  • Use the platform only for legitimate blood donation purposes.

3. Donor Eligibility

  • You must be 18–65 years old to register as a donor.
  • You must weigh at least 45 kg.
  • At least 90 days must have passed since your last donation.
  • LifeLink's eligibility check is a preliminary guide only — always consult a medical professional before donating.

4. Prohibited Activities

  • Submitting false or misleading information.
  • Attempting to hack, scrape, or abuse the platform.
  • Impersonating other users or donors.
  • Using the platform for commercial purposes.

5. Limitation of Liability

LifeLink is an educational project and does not guarantee the availability of donors, accuracy of medical information, or outcomes of blood transfusions. Always contact emergency services (112) in life-threatening situations.

6. Changes to Terms

We may update these terms periodically. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of any changes.
