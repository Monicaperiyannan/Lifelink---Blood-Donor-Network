About LifeLink

Bridging Donors and
Patients, Faster

LifeLink is a final-year project built to solve a real problem — finding a compatible, willing blood donor quickly during emergencies. We connect verified donors with hospitals, blood banks, and individuals using smart search, eligibility checks, and district-based matching.

  • Free to use for donors and recipients
  • Verified donor profiles with privacy protection
  • Built-in eligibility checker before donating
  • Emergency request system with admin review
  • Real-time analytics for blood banks and admins
